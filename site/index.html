ZollyBot

Governed, memory-driven agent offices for your repositories. Your machine runs the agents; any device runs the controls.

Install on Linux

curl -fsSL https://zollybot.shifaul.dev/install.sh | sh

The installer downloads the AppImage, verifies its SHA-256, installs ~/.local/bin/zollybot, and adds a desktop entry.
